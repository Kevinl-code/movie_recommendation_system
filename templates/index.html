Rate at least 2 movies with ★ to unlock personalised recommendations using user-based collaborative filtering.

How it works — User-based CF: Builds a sparse user×movie rating matrix, computes cosine similarity between your row and all seed users, then predicts scores for unseen films as a similarity-weighted sum of neighbour ratings. Item-based CF: Transposes the matrix to get movie×user vectors and finds films with similar rating patterns. Match % = normalised weighted score.
